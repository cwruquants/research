# CWRU HPC access

Checked against official CWRU documentation on 2026-11-08.

## What the faculty sponsor does

1. Open the AMARA link in the [CWRU Faculty Guide](https://sites.google.com/a/case.edu/hpcc/hpc-cluster/getting-started), sign in with CWRU Single Sign On and Duo, and request their own HPC account as the principal investigator (PI).
2. Once their account is set up, use AMARA to add the student or researcher to the account. If the faculty member already has an HPC account, they can proceed directly to adding the researcher. Only faculty can initiate the application; students and postdocs cannot apply independently. The faculty member can also designate senior personnel to manage account membership. These steps are documented in the [Faculty Guide](https://sites.google.com/a/case.edu/hpcc/hpc-cluster/getting-started).

The [Quickstart](https://sites.google.com/a/case.edu/hpcc/hpc-cluster/quickstart) advertises a free evaluation tier. Paid membership units expand resources; a purchase is not needed to request the free tier. Confirm the current resource limits with HPC support: the Quickstart and [account types](https://sites.google.com/a/case.edu/hpcc/hpc-cluster/getting-started/account-types) pages list different CPU limits.

## If AMARA does not work

The faculty sponsor can contact HPC support using the address on the [official UTech HPC page](https://case.edu/utech/help/knowledge-base/high-performance-computing-cluster). The [Access FAQ](https://sites.google.com/a/case.edu/hpcc/guides-and-training/faqs/accessing-hpcc) asks a PI unable to access AMARA to provide their full name, alternate CWRU email, department, phone, intended software, research funding, research description, and CaseID. Include the request to add the researcher and their CaseID as practical context.

An active CaseID is required. External collaborators first need an affiliated account, according to the [Access FAQ](https://sites.google.com/a/case.edu/hpcc/guides-and-training/faqs/accessing-hpcc).

## After provisioning

The [Quickstart researcher section](https://sites.google.com/a/case.edu/hpcc/hpc-cluster/quickstart) explains access to the Pioneer research cluster. It recommends the web-based Open OnDemand interface, which supports terminals, Jupyter, RStudio, and desktops, with CWRU credentials and Duo. That web route does not require VPN; direct SSH requires a wired campus connection or VPN.

The public instructions establish the application sequence, but the authenticated AMARA form and its exact button labels were not inspected. Follow the current form or contact HPC support if its wording differs.
